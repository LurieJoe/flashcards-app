Built for focused learning

More ways to practice the material that matters to you

Flashcard Flurry combines flexible deck creation with three study experiences and device-local privacy.

1

Make it yours

Create cards individually, paste a list, import common file formats, or start with one of 50+ built-in decks.

2

Study three ways

Use traditional flashcards, tap matching pairs, or answer locally generated multiple-choice questions.

3

Keep your place

Resume the exact card and shuffled order later, and edit a card without leaving your study session.

4

Works offline

Install it on your home screen and continue creating, studying, backing up, and restoring without a connection.

5

Private by design

No accounts, analytics, advertising, or cloud database. Your cards and study activity stay in your browser.

6

Portable backups

Create one full backup containing every profile, deck, edit, flag, preference, and saved study position.

Installable web app

Use it like an app without an app store

Open Flashcard Flurry in Safari on iPhone or iPad and choose Share → Add to Home Screen. On Android, open it in Chrome and choose Install app or Add to Home screen.

The first visit and software updates require an internet connection. Your installed study app works offline after its files have been cached.
